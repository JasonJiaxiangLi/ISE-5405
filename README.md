# ISE 5405 — Optimization I

Fall 2026 course materials for ISE 5405 at Virginia Tech, taught by
Jiaxiang (Jason) Li.

The course develops linear-programming models, geometry, theory, algorithms,
and computational methods.

- **Meetings:** Tuesdays and Thursdays, 9:30–10:45 a.m.
- **Location:** Durham Hall 261
- **Course website:** [jasonjiaxiangli.github.io/ISE-5405](https://jasonjiaxiangli.github.io/ISE-5405/)
- **Canvas:** [ISE 5405 on Canvas](https://canvas.vt.edu/courses/236227)

## Lecture materials

Open lectures from the course website for the interactive presentation. Use
the arrow keys to move between steps, press the space bar to reveal or advance,
and press `?` for the complete shortcut guide.

Each lecture also includes a downloadable PDF handout for printing or offline
reference. Materials may be updated during the semester; use Canvas as the
source of record for announcements, assignments, deadlines, and grades.

## Accessibility

The HTML slides are the primary presentation format and are designed for
keyboard, pointer, and touch use, reduced-motion preferences, and different
screen sizes. PDF downloads are noninteractive handouts and may not provide
equivalent reading order or screen-reader support.

If you encounter a barrier or need another format, contact the instructor
through Canvas.
