## GILP and other instructional references

The Simplex I materials use selected states from the GILP reference sequence:
<https://gilp.henryrobbins.com/>. GILP-derived figures, screenshots, and visual
sequences are excluded from this repository's CC BY 4.0 and MIT licenses.

The course also cites Bertsimas and Tsitsiklis, *Introduction to Linear
Optimization*, and Hildebrand, Poirrier, Bish, and Moran, *Mathematical
Programming and Operations Research*. Citations do not place third-party text,
figures, or examples under this repository's licenses.
